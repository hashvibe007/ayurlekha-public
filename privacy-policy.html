Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026  ·  App: Ayurlekha

Ayurlekha ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal health record manager. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address, used solely for authentication via one-time passcode (OTP). No password is stored.
  • Patient profiles: Name, date of birth, gender, age, height, known ailments, and medications that you enter manually.
  • Medical documents: Files you upload (lab reports, prescriptions, ECGs, doctor notes, etc.).
  • AI-generated summaries: Structured health summaries produced by our AI pipeline from your uploaded documents.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and give you access to your records.
  • To store and organise your medical documents and patient profiles.
  • To run AI analysis on uploaded documents and generate health summaries for your personal use.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond providing the app's core functionality.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in Supabase, a managed cloud platform with encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Row-Level Security (RLS) is enforced on all database tables — your records are accessible only to your authenticated account. Medical documents are stored in private, access-controlled storage buckets.

4. Who Can Access Your Data

Only you, using your authenticated account, can access your data. App administrators do not routinely access user data. No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

5. AI Processing

Uploaded documents are processed by AI models (Google Gemini) to extract medical information and generate summaries. Document contents are sent to the AI API solely for processing and are not retained by the AI provider beyond the scope of the API call, per their data usage policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete individual records or patients from within the app at any time. To request full account deletion and erasure of all associated data, contact us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Ayurlekha is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
